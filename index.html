About

I’m a mechanical engineer focused on robotic hardware, actuator design and mechatronic system integration. For my B.Sc. thesis at RUET, supervised by Prof. Md. Emdadul Haque, I designed and experimentally characterized a custom cycloidal quasi-direct-drive (QDD) joint actuator, then fabricated all twelve of them and built the 12-DoF quadruped they drive, a platform for studying locomotion on muddy terrain.

Alongside the thesis, I co-led Team Ogrodoot’s MEER 2.0 Mars rover through the finals of the Anatolian Rover Challenge 2026, where it took the competition’s highest design score, while heading its mechanical sub-team. Earlier, as an IoT hardware engineer at SystemSage Solutions, I built the entire hardware side of AgroSense, a smart-farming sensor network published at GEn-CITy 2024.

I also teach: since January 2025 I have taught 80+ classes in robotics, electronics and programming at Artificial Intelligence Bangladesh, full-time since August 2026, and written its course modules. I’m applying to M.S. and Ph.D. programs in robotics for Fall 2027, and I’m drawn to labs that design and build their own hardware.

OngoingB.Sc. thesis research · RUET

Quadruped Robot with Custom Cycloidal QDD Actuators

Thesis: Design and Development of a Quadruped Robot for Optimizing Muddy Terrain Locomotion · Supervisor: Prof. Md. Emdadul Haque

  • Designed a compact quasi-direct-drive joint actuator (BLDC motor, cycloidal reducer, encoder feedback and field-oriented control) for the torque density and backdrivability that legged locomotion needs.
  • Fabricated and assembled all twelve actuators and the robot’s full 12-DoF structure in-house.
  • Built a motor-characterization test bench (ODrive v3.6 + ESP32) whose Python pipeline time-aligns controller telemetry with output-side measurements to map efficiency.
  • Designed the platform to carry IMU and depth-camera sensing for locomotion experiments on mud.
  • Actuator design
  • Cycloidal reducer
  • BLDC · FOC
  • Fabrication
  • Legged robots
