About

I’m a mechanical engineer who builds robots from the actuator up. For my B.Sc. thesis at RUET, supervised by Prof. Md. Emdadul Haque, I designed a cycloidal quasi-direct-drive (QDD) joint actuator, then fabricated and assembled all twelve of them and the 12-DoF quadruped they drive, a platform for studying locomotion on muddy terrain.

Alongside the thesis, I co-led Team Ogrodoot’s MEER 2.0 Mars rover to the finals of the Anatolian Rover Challenge 2026, heading its mechanical sub-team. Earlier, as a hardware engineer at SystemSage Solutions, I built solar-powered IoT monitoring nodes, which led to a co-authored paper on the resulting smart-farming system at GEn-CITy 2024.

I also teach: since January 2025 I have taught 80+ classes in robotics, electronics and programming at Artificial Intelligence Bangladesh, full-time since August 2026, and written its course modules. I’m applying to M.S. and Ph.D. programs in robotics for Fall 2027, and I’m drawn to labs that design and build their own hardware.

OngoingB.Sc. thesis research · RUET

Quadruped Robot with Custom Cycloidal QDD Actuators

Thesis: Design and Development of a Quadruped Robot for Optimizing Muddy Terrain Locomotion · Supervisor: Prof. Md. Emdadul Haque

  • Designed a compact quasi-direct-drive joint actuator (BLDC motor, cycloidal reducer, encoder feedback and field-oriented control) for the torque density and backdrivability that legged locomotion needs.
  • Fabricated and assembled all twelve actuators and the robot’s full 12-DoF structure in-house.
  • Built a motor-characterization test bench (ODrive v3.6 + ESP32) whose Python pipeline time-aligns controller telemetry (velocity, current, power, faults) with output-side measurements to map efficiency.
  • Designed the platform to carry IMU and depth-camera sensing for locomotion experiments on mud and other deformable ground.
  • Actuator design
  • Cycloidal reducer
  • BLDC · FOC
  • Fabrication
  • Legged robots
