SystemSage Solutions Sdn. Bhd. · Jan 2024 – Jan 2025

AgroSense: Smart-Farming Sensor Network

Field nodes read soil nutrients and send them over LoRa to a gateway, which uploads them over 4G to a cloud dashboard; a control module switches pumps and lights manually, on range-based rules or on a schedule.

IoT Engineer (Hardware Engineering). I built the entire hardware side; teammates built the website and the Firebase receiving end.

  • Whole hardware sidePCBs, wiring, firmware and solar power
  • LoRa + 4Gstar network to a gateway, 4G upload to the cloud
  • Three control modesmanual, range-based rules, schedule
  • GEn-CITy 2024published paper [P1]
01

How it works

Child Modules, the field nodes, read a 7-in-1 soil sensor (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH, moisture, temperature and conductivity) and send the readings over LoRa to a Mother Module, the gateway, in a star network. The gateway uploads them over 4G to a cloud database behind the web dashboard.

Some of the data also goes to Firebase, where a Control Module picks it up and switches equipment such as pumps or lights through relays: manually from the website, automatically when a reading leaves a set range, or on a schedule. The field nodes run on solar power with battery backup.

Hardware: ESP32 · LoRa · SIMCom A7670C (4G) · RS485 7-in-1 soil sensor · BME280 · custom PCBs · 4-channel relay module · solar panel, battery and charge controller.
