Quadruped Robot with Custom Cycloidal QDD Actuators

A 12-DoF quadruped for studying locomotion on muddy terrain, driven by twelve cycloidal quasi-direct-drive actuators that I designed and fabricated in-house.

Thesis: Design and Development of a Quadruped Robot for Optimizing Muddy Terrain Locomotion · Supervisor: Prof. Md. Emdadul Haque, Department of Mechanical Engineering, RUET

  • 12 actuatorscustom cycloidal QDD, fabricated in-house
  • 12-DoF structurefour legs, three actuated joints each, assembled
  • Test benchODrive v3.6 + ESP32, time-aligned Python logging
  • Next: mudlocomotion experiments on muddy terrain
